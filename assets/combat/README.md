# 战斗插画

这一组原创插画由内置 ImageGen 生成。战斗视图从左前景的诺缇雅肩后看向右前方的敌人。前景与敌人使用带 Alpha 通道的 PNG，远景铺满舞台。缺图时由 GDScript 绘制替代图。

| 素材 | 生成提示 |
|---|---|
| `crimson_wolf.png` | A single crimson rock wolf from a fantasy badlands ecosystem, jagged russet slate plates on its shoulders, amber eyes and a crouching front-three-quarter pose. Hand-painted Japanese fantasy anime in gouache, warm pale rim light, full figure with paws and tail, transparent background. |
| `sword_god_phantom.png` | An original cool jade-and-ivory stone phantom of an adult sword master, full figure with a simple fantasy blade held low, pale teal robes, chipped stone and jade wisps attached to the silhouette. Hand-painted Japanese light-novel illustration on transparent background. |
| `notia_young_shoulder.png` | Ten-year-old Notia seen from behind, with a small indigo braid, oatmeal linen and a brown travel mantle. The camera is close behind her left shoulder. She faces screen right. Chest-up character silhouette cropped against the left and lower edges, painterly anime gouache with golden rim light, transparent background. |
| `notia_adult_shoulder.png` | Adult Notia seen from behind her left shoulder, with a long indigo braid and a deep teal mage cloak with cream embroidery. Her back and shoulder fill the left foreground and she faces screen right. Chest-up hand-painted anime silhouette, transparent background. |
| `../backgrounds/combat_wilderness.png` | Wide eye-level ochre badlands clearing with an open middle, ochre precipices at the sides and a softly layered violet horizon. Warm Japanese anime gouache, no characters or interface. |
| `../backgrounds/combat_stone_trial.png` | Wide eye-level ancient stone trial courtyard overlooking a dry mountain valley. Jade-gray standing stones, open central flagstones, distant cracked monument and ivory sunset. Textured anime gouache, no characters or interface. |

生成的前景素材由视图按透明区裁切。敌人素材保留独立的轮廓、投影和距离缩放。更新素材路径可修改 [预设](../../data/combat/presets.json) 与 [敌人配置](../../data/combat/enemies.json)。
